# Impeccable design context — Fuin v2

> Inferred from the architecture spec at `Growth/ideas/2026-05-04-fuin-v2-proof-of-intent-architecture.md` and the v1 codebase at `apps/vault/`. User explicitly delegated context choices.

## Design Context

### Users
**Primary:** Frontier hackathon judges (Solana Foundation, Anatoly Yakovenko, technical reviewers) browsing project submissions on a laptop, possibly between many entries. They have ~90 seconds to form an impression. They are sophisticated about Solana protocol design and skeptical of marketing copy.

**Secondary:** AI agent infrastructure builders (SendAI/Eliza users, Helius integrators) evaluating Fuin v2 as a dependency for swarm orchestration. They want to know: how do I integrate this, what are the primitives, what's the trust model.

**Tertiary (post-hack):** Institutional buyers — CFOs of crypto-native firms, syndicate operators, DAO ops contributors — who would audit AI-agent spend through Fuin.

### Brand Personality
**Three words: rigorous, architectural, ledger-quiet.**

- *Rigorous* — every action has a cryptographic chain of custody back to a root signature; the math denies misuse. Voice never claims more than the math proves.
- *Architectural* — hierarchical, structural, composable. This is infrastructure-class. Not a product, a substrate.
- *Ledger-quiet* — auditable but not screaming. Institutional credibility comes from understatement.

The brand should feel closer to a regulatory filing or a Bloomberg terminal manual than to a startup landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction

**References (yes):**
- Bloomberg terminal manuals on quality paper
- 1970s mainframe documentation covers (IBM, DEC)
- The inside cover of a financial regulatory filing
- A mathematical proof typeset in a journal (e.g., AMS papers)
- Archival drawer-card systems

**Anti-references (no):**
- The 2024 AI startup landing page (big gradient hero, three icon-feature cards, "trusted by" logo strip)
- The Solana neon ecosystem aesthetic (cyan glow on dark, gradient shadows)
- The Vercel-clone (Geist + dark, every dev tool from 2023)
- Glassmorphism, glow accents, gradient text — already on impeccable's ban list

**Theme:** Dark. The product is consumed by traders, fund operators, AI infra developers in long sessions on monitors — dark is the right context. But not "AI dark." Background should be a deep ink-blue-black (not pure #000), with warm cream-tinted text (not pure #fff). One saturated accent reserved for ~10% of pixels.

**Color (OKLCH):**
- Background: oklch(0.16 0.014 230) — deep ink-blue-black
- Surface (one step up): oklch(0.20 0.012 230)
- Border / muted line: oklch(0.30 0.014 230)
- Primary text: oklch(0.94 0.008 80) — warm cream
- Secondary text: oklch(0.68 0.012 230) — muted ink
- Accent: oklch(0.80 0.17 92) — archival ledger-yellow (NOT cyan, NOT purple, NOT neon)
- Critical/revoked: oklch(0.55 0.18 30) — oxidized brick (rare, used only for revoked/danger states)

**Typography (selected via the procedure in impeccable, NOT from the reflex-reject list):**
- Display: **Vollkorn** (Google) — robust serif with weight, editorial-architectural, used for hero and section headers
- Body / UI: **Schibsted Grotesk** (Google) — Norwegian newspaper sans, warm but rigorous, used for body and labels
- Mono: **Departure Mono** (Google) — pixel-archival mainframe feel, used for code, addresses, signatures, intent IDs

Fluid scale on marketing surfaces (clamp); fixed rem scale on product surfaces (audit dashboard). 1.25 ratio between steps. Cap body to 65ch.

**Spacing:** 4pt scale. Asymmetric layouts on marketing pages — left-aligned text, generous breathing room between sections, varied vertical rhythm. Product page (audit dashboard) uses a tighter 8pt-derived card system.

### Design Principles

1. **Show the math, not the metaphor.** Let cryptographic detail speak. Don't dress proof up in trust-fall stock photos.
2. **One signature, hierarchical scope.** Visualize the thing the protocol *is* — a tree of intents — as a first-class on-page element, not buried in a feature card.
3. **Restraint earns the accent.** The single archival-yellow accent should land on no more than one element per viewport. Power comes from rarity.
4. **Body type for thinking, mono type for proof.** Whenever an on-chain artifact is named (signature, PDA, program ID), it goes in mono. The reader should be able to copy/paste anything that exists on-chain.
5. **No feature cards.** No three-column grid of icon + heading + paragraph. That layout is the AI-slop fingerprint. Replace with editorial sections and asymmetric compositions.
